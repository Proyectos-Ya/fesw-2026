Square icon-only button for toolbars and cards. Always pass an accessible `label`.

```jsx
<IconButton icon={<Icon name="bell" size={20} />} label="Notificaciones" variant="ghost" />
<IconButton icon={<Icon name="bookmark" size={18} />} label="Guardar licitación" />
```

Variants `primary | secondary | ghost`; sizes `sm | md | lg`. Press = scale 0.94.
